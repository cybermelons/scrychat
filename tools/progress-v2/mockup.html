Sefris of the Hidden Ways

W U B Esper reanimator · 100 cards · €142.80
Strategy
Fill the graveyard with creature cards so Sefris ventures once each turn. Completing dungeons triggers Create Undead and returns creatures for free. Because the venture trigger is capped per turn, the deck wants creatures hitting the yard on every player's turn — so instant-speed reanimation fits the rhythm.

Six cards whose own ability puts a creature from the graveyard onto the battlefield at instant speed?

All six do it on their own, at instant speed — which matters here because Sefris only ventures once per turn, so you want creatures hitting the yard on everyone's turn.

6 cards · instant-speed reanimation
Necromancy{3}{B}
Enchantment, flash. Return target creature from a graveyard to the battlefield under your control.
Coffin Queen{2}{B}
Tap: exile target creature card from a graveyard, put it onto the battlefield under your control.
